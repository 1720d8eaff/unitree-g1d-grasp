# Version 0.3.8

Bounded operator-reviewed target-drift acceptance for the saved grasp cycle.

- `--accept-reviewed-target-drift` is available only with
  `--execute-saved --grasp-cycle` and an interactive full-path RViz review.
- It raises the saved-to-live target limits to 15 mm translation and 20 degrees
  of approach-axis change; larger differences still require a fresh plan.
- The target is sampled once before publishing the combined main-plus-lift
  preview and once after the operator confirmation. The redundant third sample
  after PREOPEN was removed.
- Collision, table, saved-artifact hash, joint start, wrist range, trajectory
  timing, main endpoint, and lift endpoint checks are unchanged.
